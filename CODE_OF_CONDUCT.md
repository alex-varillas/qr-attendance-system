# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful and welcoming place to ask questions, report problems and contribute, whatever your background, experience, language or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Be specific and constructive when you disagree: argue about the idea, not the person.
- Accept that people write in different languages and have different levels of experience. Help instead of correcting.
- Respect other people's time and privacy. Do not share private information about anyone, and do not post real employee or business data in issues.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or attention, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict someone's participation when needed. To report a problem, use the private reporting option in the **Security** tab of the repository and start the report with "Conduct". Reports are handled privately and with respect for the people involved.

## Scope

This code applies to the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to the people representing the project in public.
